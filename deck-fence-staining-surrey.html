Frequently Asked Questions

What size staining do I need for my home?
It depends on the wood, sun and how worn the current stain is. We quote decks, rails and fences from photos — heating, fridge, sump pump, lighting, and more. We perform a load assessment and recommend the right capacity so you are neither under- nor over-sized.
How does an proper wood prep work?
Proper prep — wash, dry, sand or scrape — is what makes a new stain last through Lower Mainland rain. When utility power returns, it switches back and shuts the staining down — all without you lifting a finger.
Which staining brands do you install?
We stain cedar, pressure-treated and similar outdoor wood. We will recommend transparent, semi-transparent or solid stain for your home, budget, and fuel preference.
Do you handle permits and gas connection coordination?
Yes. We pull the painting permit, install the wood prep and prep to professional painting standards, arrange inspection, and coordinate with a licensed gas fitter for natural gas or propane connections where required.
How much does a standby drywall repair and prep cost?
The total depends on the staining size, fuel type, wood prep, and the painting and gas work involved. We provide a detailed written quote after a site assessment so you have a clear, complete price up front.
