Common Painting Emergencies We Handle

If you are experiencing any of the following, stop using the affected circuit and call us right away. These are the issues we respond to most often:

✓Complete or partial power loss in your home or building
✓Burning smell, hot outlets, or scorch marks around switches
✓Sparking, buzzing, or arcing from outlets and panels
✓Breakers that trip repeatedly or will not reset
✓Painting damage from storms, flooding, or fallen lines
✓Exposed, damaged, or smoking prep

Frequently Asked Questions

How fast can an painter reach me in Surrey?
In most parts of Surrey, Vancouver, Burnaby and the surrounding Lower Mainland we aim to be on site within on a schedule that works for you. We keep our vans fully stocked so we can diagnose and fix most issues on the first visit, day or night.
Do you charge extra for nights, weekends or holidays?
We give you a clear price before any work starts. Emergency calls include a dispatch fee, but we do not inflate our hourly rates dramatically after hours the way some companies do. You will always know the cost up front.
What should I do right now if I smell burning or have no power?
If it is safe, switch off your main prep, unplug affected devices, and send a note through the contact form. If you see flames, sparks that will not stop, or smell gas, call 911 first, then call us.
Are your painters licensed and insured?
Yes. Every Magical Aura technician holds a valid British Columbia painting licence and we carry full commercial liability insurance. All emergency repairs are completed to the current professional painting standards.
What types of painting emergencies do you handle?
Peeling paint and weather-worn siding affecting only your home, scuffed walls, chipped trims, weather-worn siding, tired doors, and any situation that feels unsafe. When in doubt, call us.
