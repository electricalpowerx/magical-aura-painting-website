Quoting FAQ

Do you publish a price list?
No. Every interior, exterior, cabinet or commercial job is quoted in writing after we see the space. Photos through the form are a good start.
Are quotes free?
Yes. Use the contact form. A phone number is coming soon.
What affects a painting quote?
Room count, ceiling height, surface condition, colour changes, exterior access, and whether drywall repair or popcorn work is needed. We explain that in the written quote.
Are there hidden fees?
No. You approve a written quote before work begins.
How do I get started?
Send a few photos and a note through the contact form. We follow up with next steps and a free written quote.
