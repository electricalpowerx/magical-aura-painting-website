Frequently Asked Questions

What does a cabinet painting quote include in Surrey?
Cost depends on how many doors and drawers, the current finish, and the current sheen. A small vanity is a shorter visit; a full kitchen cost more. We give a free, fixed quote after a quick site assessment.
Do you paint laminate cabinets?
Often yes, with the right primer. We confirm from photos. A full interior, if you want one, is quoted separately and is quoted separately. If doors need extra sanding we will tell you honestly and quote both options.
Which cabinets do you install?
We paint kitchen and bathroom cabinets — doors, drawers and frames. Send photos through the form and we will recommend the best finish for your kitchen, panel and budget.
Are there rebates for cabinets in BC?
Rebate programs through your schedule and other agencies change periodically. We stay current on what is available and help you choose equipment that qualifies, but we recommend confirming the latest rebate details directly with the program before purchase.
Do you handle the permit for cabinet painting?
Yes. We pull the painting permit, install to the current professional painting standards, and arrange the professional workmanship inspection so the finish holds up to daily kitchen use. ready to use.
