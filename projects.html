Frequently Asked Questions

How long does a typical painting project take?
A single room can often be finished in a day or two. Whole-home interiors usually take several days depending on prep, colours and drying. Cabinets and exteriors are scheduled after a written quote.
Do you protect floors and furniture?
Yes. Clean prep is part of every job: floors, furniture and fixtures are protected, and we leave the space tidy when we are done.
What kind of warranty do you offer?
We stand behind our workmanship. Quality materials and careful prep are standard; if something is not right, we make it right.
Can I see more examples before booking?
Our gallery is expanding. When you request a quote, tell us about your space and we can share finishes and process details that match what you need.
Do you provide updates on multi-day projects?
Yes. For multi-day interiors or exteriors we keep you informed so you always know what is happening next.
