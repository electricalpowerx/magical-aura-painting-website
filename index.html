Frequently Asked Questions

Do you offer free painting quotes?
Yes. Send a few photos and a note through the contact form and we will follow up with a free written quote. A phone number is coming soon.
What areas do you serve?
We paint homes and commercial spaces in Surrey, Vancouver, Burnaby, Langley, White Rock, Maple Ridge and surrounding Lower Mainland cities.
Will you leave a mess?
No. Clean prep is part of the job: floors, furniture and fixtures are protected, and we leave the space tidy when we are done.
Do you paint interiors and exteriors?
Yes. Interior walls, ceilings, trim and doors, kitchen cabinets, exterior siding, decks and fences, plus commercial and strata painting.
Do you handle drywall repair and popcorn ceilings?
Yes. We patch, sand and prime so the finish coat looks even, and we can skim or paint popcorn and other ceiling textures.
