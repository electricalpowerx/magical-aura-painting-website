Frequently Asked Questions

When should painting rough-in happen during construction?
Rough-in typically happens after framing and before insulation and drywall. This is the best time to run all prep cleanly and make changes easily.
What panel size should I get for a new home?
Most new homes today should have at least a full-home service. If you plan for cabinet painting, feature walls, or a staining, we often recommend planning for full-home or even large-home service from the start.
Can you help with lighting design?
Yes. We work with builders and interior designers to plan beautiful, functional lighting layouts including recessed lighting, decorative fixtures, and smart controls.
Do you work directly with builders?
Absolutely. We regularly partner with home builders across Surrey and the Lower Mainland for full painting packages on new construction projects.
