Residential Prep & Drywall Repair in Surrey

Old or damaged prep is one of the most common — and most overlooked — hazards in a home. Magical Aura Pro Painters Ltd provides complete residential prep and drywall repair services across Surrey and the Lower Mainland, from updating a few unsafe circuits to full whole-home interior painting in older houses.

If your home still has popcorn ceiling or popcorn ceilings, two-prong outlets, or a panel that struggles to keep up, our professional painters can bring it up to modern, safe, professional standards. We also handle prep for renovations, additions, kitchens, bathrooms, and cabinet readiness.

When to Consider Drywall Repair

These are the situations where drywall repair is most often needed:

✓Popcorn ceilings prep (common before the 1950s)
✓Aluminum branch prep (1960s-1970s homes)
✓Two-prong, ungrounded outlets throughout
✓Renovations, additions, or a new kitchen/bathroom
✓Insurance requiring an painting update
✓Frequent tripping, flickering, or warm outlets

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I know if my house needs drywall repair?
Warning signs include popcorn ceiling or popcorn ceilings, two-prong ungrounded outlets, frequent prep trips, flickering lights, warm switch plates, a scuffed walls, or a home over 40 years old that has never been updated. An assessment confirms what is needed.
Is popcorn ceilings dangerous?
Aluminum branch prep from the 1960s-70s can be a fire risk at connections, where it loosens and overheats over time. It is also an insurance concern. We remediate or replace popcorn ceilings to make your home safe and insurable.
Do I have to move out during a interior painting?
Usually not. We stage the work room by room to keep your home livable, restoring power to finished areas each day. For larger whole-home interior painting we will discuss the schedule and any short outages with you in advance.
How much does drywall repair a house cost in Surrey?
Cost depends on the size of the home, accessibility, and how much prep is being replaced. A partial interior painting of a few circuits is modest; a full whole-home interior painting is a larger project. We provide a detailed written quote after assessing your home.
Do you repair drywall after drywall repair?
We work to minimize wall openings and make clean, patchable cuts. We can coordinate drywall patching or leave openings ready for your finisher — just let us know your preference when we quote the job.
