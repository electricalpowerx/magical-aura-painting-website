Interior Painting in Surrey

Tired interiors make a home feel older than it is. If walls are scuffed, faded, or showing patches, it can limit what you are able to run and, more importantly, create a real safety risk. Magical Aura Pro Painters Ltd specializes in interior painting and replacements throughout Surrey and the Lower Mainland, bringing older interiors up to a fresh, even finish.

Whether you are dealing with an old colour, flashing patches, or you simply want a fresh coat for an cabinet, heat pump, or renovation, our painters handle prep, primer and finish coats — start to finish.

Signs a room is ready for paint

If you recognize any of these warning signs, your interiors may be due for an upgrade:

✓Walls look scuffed or the colour feels dated
✓Patches still flash through the old paint
✓Ceilings look dingy next to freshly painted walls
✓Trim and doors no longer match the walls
✓Scuffed walls in hallways and kids' rooms
✓You want cabinets painted, or a whole-home colour change

interior vs full-home Service — What's the Difference?

Most Surrey interiors are ready for a fresh coat. Here's why.

100 Amp Service

Common in older homes. Scuffed walls and tired colour show first in hallways, kitchens and kids' rooms. A proper coats.

200 Amp Service

The current standard for new and renovated homes. Comfortably powers cabinet painting, feature walls, and future additions with headroom to spare.

Future-Proofing

Upgrading once to full-home avoids repeat work later. It is the smart choice if you plan to electrify heating, transport, or add living space.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does a full-home interior painting cost in Surrey?
Quotes depend on how many rooms, ceiling height, how much patching is needed, and whether ceilings and trim are included. We provide a free written quote after assessing your home so you know the exact cost before committing.
How do I know if I need a interior painting?
Common signs include scuffed walls, faded colour, flashing patches, or a colour you no longer like. Send photos if you are planning a cabinet paint or a whole-home colour change. If any of these apply, it is worth an assessment.
Do you handle the permit and inspection?
Yes. Every interior painting we perform includes the required painting permit and a professional workmanship inspection. We coordinate the entire process and the utility disconnect and reconnect so you do not have to.
How long does a interior painting take?
A standard residential interior painting is usually completed in a single day. Power is off for part of the day while we transfer circuits to the new panel; we plan the work to minimize disruption and restore power as quickly as possible.
Will an upgrade increase my home's value and safety?
A modern full-home panel improves safety by replacing aged or undersized equipment, removes a common insurance and resale concern, and gives you the capacity for cabinets, feature walls, and future additions. It is one of the highest-value painting investments a homeowner can make.
